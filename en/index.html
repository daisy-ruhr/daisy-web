DAISY¶

Safe use of AI in companies Data Protection and Employee Interests in AI-as-a-Service Systems

AI has become an important tool in many companies.
DAISY aims to research service solutions that are practical while minimizing privacy risks: technically, psychologically and organizationally, tested in everyday business operations.

Project summary¶

Many companies use artificial intelligence as a service: employees query an internally configured chatbot whose language model is operated in the cloud by a third-party provider. This is cheap, fast, and requires hardly any in-house AI expertise. In other enterprises, an AI strategy might not even have been laid out yet and employees are using various unpoliced personal tools.

What remains open is what happens to employees' data, who bears responsibility and where the limits of the system lie - especially when automating workflows with agentic tools. These risks are rarely intuitive for users and decision-makers, as they differ significantly from the commercial chatbots people know from their private lives.

DAISY starts exactly there. As a consortium of computer science, psychology and practice, we develop IT security solutions, strengthen the competence of decision-makers and employees, and shape the introduction of AI services in Ruhr area companies together in a participatory and transparent way. The legal framework is taken into account from the very beginning.

Latest news¶

All news

  • Secure technology

    We build on open-source tools and test configurations that address typical privacy risks of AIaaS. Interactive demonstrators make them tangible.

  • Sound insights

    We investigate how employees and decision-makers understand AIaaS, and compare these mental models with the actual properties of the systems.

  • Practical processes

    We develop evidence-based process models for a calibrated, employee-centered introduction of AI services, tailored to the organizational culture.

  • Free use

    We make all results available under an open license and free of charge. No separate patent or property right applications are planned.
