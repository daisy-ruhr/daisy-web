Accessibility Statement¶

Courtesy translation

This is a courtesy translation. The German version of this page is legally binding.

Ruhr University Bochum, as consortium lead of the DAISY project, is committed to making its website accessible in accordance with the national legislation transposing Directive (EU) 2016/2102 of the European Parliament and of the Council.

This accessibility statement applies to the website of the DAISY project, which is available in German and English at https://daisy.ruhr/.

Compliance status¶

This website is partially compliant with the federal and state disability equality acts (BGG & LGG), the Accessible Information Technology Ordinance (BITV) and the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1.

Reasons¶

The content listed below is not accessible for the following reasons:

Non-compliance with the requirements:

  • Headings are followed by a link symbol that links directly to the section. Screen readers announce it as "¶", and its tooltip ("Permanent link") is in English on the German pages as well (WCAG 2.4.4, 3.1.2).

We are working on removing these barriers.

Preparation of this accessibility statement¶

This statement was prepared on 25 November 2026.

The assessment is based on a self-evaluation. All pages were tested automatically with axe-core in light and dark colour schemes at desktop and smartphone widths, and intuitive keyboard navigation was verified manually.

The statement was last reviewed on 25 November 2026.

Feedback and contact information¶

Ruhr University Bochum is working on removing the existing barriers.

If you

  • encounter barriers while using the website,
  • find content that is difficult to access,
  • find content that violates general accessibility recommendations or does not conform to WCAG,
  • find content that is unclear and should be expressed or worded differently,
  • or have other questions about how accessibility is implemented,

please contact us by e-mail at:

digital-barrierefrei@ruhr-uni-bochum.de

The University Communications Department (Dezernat Hochschulkommunikation) is your first point of contact and will forward your request to the responsible units.

Enforcement procedure¶

If you have not received a satisfactory response to your notification or request within six weeks, you can contact the ombuds office for accessible information technology (Ombudsstelle für barrierefreie Informationstechnik). It is attached to the Commissioner for the Concerns of People with Disabilities under § 11 of the North Rhine-Westphalian Disability Equality Act (Behindertengleichstellungsgesetz Nordrhein-Westfalen). If you would like to initiate an ombuds procedure, please fill in the application form (PDF, in German) and send it by e-mail to the ombuds office at ombudsstelle-barrierefreie-it@mags.nrw.de.
