# Legal Framework

At DAISY, legal requirements are not an afterthought but part of the starting point: they
form the guardrails within which technical, psychological and organizational solutions are
developed.

For companies – especially small and medium-sized ones without in-house legal expertise –
the situation is hard to oversee. The project aims to identify legal guardrails, gray areas
and prerequisites and make them manageable. The findings are closely interlinked with the
other project results.

## The legal opinion

DAISY commissions a legal opinion externally as a service contract, awarded via RUB and
accompanied by it on a professional level.

* **Focus:** The Digital Services Act and its interplay with labor law.
* **Contextualization:** The opinion embeds the DSA in the other relevant legal frameworks.
* **Result:** Identified guardrails, gray areas and prerequisites for the use of AIaaS –
  prepared so that companies can work with them.

!!! info "Status"
    The opinion is being prepared within the project (milestone M3). An application-oriented
    version for decision-makers follows as a separate result (milestone M19). This page will
    be updated as soon as the results are available.

## Relevant legal frameworks

<div class="grid cards" markdown="1">

- __General Data Protection Regulation__

    ---

    Basis for any processing of personal data – and thus for every input that employees
    send to an AI service.

- __Federal Data Protection Act__

    ---

    Supplements the GDPR in national law, among other things for employee data in the
    employment relationship.

- __Works Constitution Act__

    ---

    Establishes co-determination rights of works councils – for example when introducing
    systems that can monitor employees' behavior or performance.

- __Occupational Safety and Health Act__

    ---

    Covers the protection of employees' health – and thus also psychological strain from
    data-driven management in the work context.

- __Digital Services Act__

    ---

    Regulates obligations for digital services in the EU and is the focus of the DAISY
    legal opinion, in particular in its interplay with labor law.

- __EU AI Act__

    ---

    Introduces risk-based obligations for AI systems – with considerable significance for
    the introduction of AI in companies.

</div>

!!! warning "No legal advice"
    This page provides an overview of the project's work on legal questions. It is not legal
    advice. For specific cases, please contact the responsible data protection officer, the
    works council or a lawyer.
