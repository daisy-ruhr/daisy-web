# Scientific Approach

DAISY regards the introduction of AI services in organizations as a
**sociotechnical system**: technology and people are inseparably linked, and data
protection only succeeds if both are considered together.

To this end, the consortium works inter- and transdisciplinarily. This allows, on the one
hand, the separate examination of individual system elements and levels and, on the other,
their integration into a holistic model of human-centered, privacy-sensitive and practically
implementable AIaaS introductions.

## The three levels of analysis

<div class="grid cards" markdown="1">

- __Micro level__

    ---

    AIaaS systems and their characteristics are analyzed and developed as demonstrators
    (RUB). In parallel, we examine employees' subjective mental models and their personal
    conditions for use (UDE).

- __Meso level__

    ---

    Organizational structures come into focus: decision-makers are interviewed, and
    processes for privacy-sensitive AI introduction are examined, optimized and prepared
    for use (UW/H).

- __Macro level__

    ---

    The legal framework for AIaaS introductions and the aspect of privacy in
    organizational application are examined (externally commissioned legal opinion).
    In addition, we look at implementability and transfer to companies in various
    sectors – with the aim of strengthening Germany as a business location (BoWE).

</div>

## Partner perspectives

=== "RUB – Technology"

    Ruhr University Bochum focuses on the **technical subsystem**. It compiles the state of
    research on security and data protection architectures of AIaaS systems, empirically
    analyzes practically relevant systems and assesses them with regard to their data
    protection. Building on this, it defines technical requirements for data-protection-
    compliant configurations.

    Consortium coordination: RUB steers the overall project and also coordinates the
    commissioning of the external legal opinion.

=== "UDE – People"

    The University of Duisburg-Essen examines the **social subsystem**. Its goal is the
    systematic, theory-driven capture of the mental models of AIaaS users. These are
    compared with system characteristics in order to identify discrepancies. The
    intervention formats developed and evaluated reduce misconceptions among employees and
    strengthen individual usage skills.

=== "UW/H – Organization"

    Witten/Herdecke University focuses on the **organizational link** between the social
    and the technical subsystem. It investigates decision-making and negotiation processes
    during AIaaS introduction, identifies decision criteria and perspectives of key actors,
    and compares them with system characteristics in order to work out blind spots.

    Its central result is evidence-based process models for expectation-calibrated AIaaS
    introductions, tailored to organizational culture and existing expectations of fairness.

=== "BoWE – Practice"

    WirtschaftsEntwicklungsGesellschaft Bochum mbH contributes **application and transfer
    expertise**. The focus is on a regional view of privacy-sensitive AI concepts in
    organizational application, which complements and makes concrete the broader scientific
    findings of the partners.

    BoWE records legal and practical problem areas in AIaaS introductions, supports the
    scientific work, continuously reflects on it using the case example, and enables the
    evaluation of the solutions developed.

## Technical objectives

At the technical level, DAISY pursues two goals:

* A **technical demonstrator** for a data-protection-compliant AIaaS solution is to be made
  available. From existing open-source tools we develop demonstrator configurations that
  address typical data protection risks. They are evaluated, optimized and prepared as
  interactive demonstrators – making the technical component tangible.
* A corresponding solution is **operationally implemented** at BoWE as the practice partner
  and tested in trial operation. In addition to further evaluation, the aim is transfer in
  the form of technical recommendations and recommendations for action for other companies.

## Overarching goals

**Early-career researchers.** Within the project's scope, doctoral degrees are made
possible. This promotes Germany as a location for education and science, and the economy
through practice-oriented innovation.

**Science communication.** Findings and solutions reach decision-makers and employees via
this project website and via events in companies. Privacy sensitivity is not only to be
studied theoretically, but supported practically in organizations.

**Legal guardrails.** An externally commissioned legal opinion clarifies the conditions for
a use of AIaaS that complies with data protection and employee interests. It examines in
particular the Digital Services Act and its interplay with labor law, and places it in the
context of other relevant legal frameworks. More on the page
[Legal Framework](recht.md).

## Prior work: a joint field study

A key foundation of DAISY is a [joint field study by RUB and UDE](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3772318.3790622):
the perception of an LLM chatbot implemented at a university was examined with more than 500
users in comparison to ChatGPT. Among other things, the institutional system was met with
higher trust and higher perceived privacy.

At the same time, the study demonstrates the team's ability to conduct interdisciplinary
field studies in the as-a-service setting.
