## Time horizons

The project period spans three years: 1 September 2026 to 31 August 2029. The impact
extends beyond that.

=== "Short term"

    During and immediately after the project period, BoWE benefits directly as the pilot
    user – through efficiency gains and greater legal certainty in AI use. Risk profiles,
    technical requirements, process models and decision aids enable organizations to assess
    AIaaS offerings in a structured way and to configure existing systems in compliance with
    data protection. This avoids compliance risks and follow-up costs.

    Empirically validated training and assessment formats are shared with partner companies
    already during the project and carried to further companies via the multipliers IG
    Metall and HUMAINE Network e.V.

=== "Medium term"

    The freely available risk profiles, configuration guides, decision tools and governance
    and qualification concepts strengthen in particular the competitiveness of small and
    medium-sized enterprises without their own IT security or data protection department.
    Public administrations can use them as orientation for AI introduction in regulated
    contexts.

    Employees and their representatives – works councils, data protection officers – receive
    a reliable basis for informed participation.

=== "Long term"

    With increasing regulatory density, for example through the EU AI Act, the
    evidence-based foundation gains additional importance: it supports organizations in the
    technically sound implementation of regulatory requirements and creates the conditions
    for standards and best-practice recommendations for trustworthy AI use in the world of
    work.

    In this way, the project contributes to the digital sovereignty and competitiveness of
    Germany as a business location – including a regional pioneering role via BoWE and the
    City of Bochum group.

## Scientific dissemination

The results are disseminated at peer-reviewed conferences and in journals – in usable
security & privacy and IT security as well as in psychological and human-technology
research. The aim is both independent contributions from the sub-projects and
interdisciplinary joint publications that bring together technical risk profiles with
mental models and implementation processes.
Scientific publications are listed on the [Publications](publications.md) page.

Through the sub-projects, several doctorates as well as project-related theses are made
possible, embedded in interdisciplinary structures – including the graduate school of the
RC Trust, and the cooperation with the Cluster of Excellence CASA and the MPI-SP.

## Transfer into practice

The economic impact arises indirectly: the results enable companies and public
organizations in Germany to use AIaaS in a data-protection-compliant and employee-centered
way. This transfer is carried by BoWE as practice partner and multiplier as well as by
the associated partners – IG Metall into employee representation, HUMAINE Network e.V.
into industry, Edgeless Systems into product development. Details on the page
[Consortium & Network](network.md).
