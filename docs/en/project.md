# The Project

DAISY minimizes the data protection risks of AI services that companies purchase as a
service (AI-as-a-Service, AIaaS), systematically taking employees' interests into account.

## Why AIaaS is a data protection issue

The use of AI in companies is growing strongly, and cloud-based solutions that provide AI
models "on demand" are becoming ever more relevant. Instead of developing and training their
own models, employees access third-party language models, for example via an internally
configured chatbot.

This allows organizations to introduce AI cost-effectively – even without extensive
technical expertise. However, it entails particular risks for the privacy and data of
employees. The systems largely elude the direct control of companies and employees; the
risks arise from the architecture of the models, from operational implementations and from
dependence on third-party providers.

DAISY therefore focuses on IT security solutions and measures to promote competence – in
order to protect citizens' privacy where they spend a large part of their day: at work.

## Goals

!!! info "Core idea"
    Bringing the mental models of AIaaS on the decision-maker and user side together with
    the actual properties of the systems while preserving privacy and performance. This is the
    challenge the entire project is built around.

Specifically, DAISY addresses five goals:

1. **Provide technological solutions** for secure and data-protection-compliant AIaaS
   integration.
2. **Empower decision-makers** to design AIaaS in a privacy-preserving, transparent,
   secure and employee-centered way.
3. **Empower employees** to handle their individual data safely when using AIaaS.
4. **Develop strategies** for secure, privacy-preserving and human-centered implementation
   processes – including better communication about AI functionalities and data
   protection in organizations.
5. **Test and evaluate solutions** and make them usable for employees.

## Our Approach

Data protection law is taken into account from the start and forms the framework for
technical, individual-psychological and organizational solutions. Thereby, the project
reconciles the economic interests of German companies with the psychological needs of
employees and legal interests worthy of protection.

Small and medium-sized enterprises are of particular importance: they usually have few
resources for the strategic use of AI and depend on practical, affordable support.

## Relation to the funding objectives

The project contributes to the strategic goal "Privacy and data protection: self-determined
and innovative". It addresses citizens' informational self-determination in a context in
which a lot of data is generated and self-determination is complemented by externally
determined aspects: the workplace.

Organizational actors today no longer have to understand only "classic" AI risks, but must
also be able to assess dangers arising from the architecture of the models themselves, from
operational implementations like agentic frameworks or from dependence on foreign third-party providers. This requires adequate cognitive frameworks for interpreting how the systems work, their limits
and their risks – which are rarely present on the user and decision-maker side.

DAISY strengthens awareness of data protection, privacy and employee orientation, and thus
contributes to the capacity for self-determination – as well as to appropriate
external determination of data protection by the employer. The technical solutions and
organizational implementation processes developed implement European data protection through
"privacy by design".
