# Glossary

The most important terms of the project – explained briefly and without jargon.

**AIaaS – AI-as-a-Service**
: AI functionality that a company does not develop itself but purchases from a provider as a
  cloud-based service – typically "on demand" via interfaces or an internally configured
  chatbot.

**LLMaaS – LLM-as-a-Service**
: Special case of AIaaS in which a large language model (LLM) is provided as a service, for
  example a chatbot based on a commercial language model.

**Mental model**
: The inner picture people form of a system – how it works, what happens to their data, who
  is responsible. With AIaaS it often deviates from the actual system properties.

**Calibration / expectation-calibrated**
: A state in which the expectations of users and decision-makers match the real
  possibilities and limits of the system. DAISY aims at expectation-calibrated
  introductions – that is, realistic expectations, neither inflated nor too carefree.

**Privacy / informational self-determination**
: The right to determine what happens to one's own personal data. In the workplace this
  right is limited because the employer introduces the systems – which is why DAISY also
  speaks of appropriate external determination by the employer.

**Privacy by design**
: Approach of anchoring data protection as a design requirement in technology and
  processes instead of adding it afterwards.

**Sociotechnical approach**
: A perspective that considers the technical and the social system together: technology
  affects people, people shape technology. Data protection problems cannot be solved from
  either perspective alone.

**Risk profile**
: Compact, comparative presentation of the data-protection-relevant characteristics of an
  AIaaS system – as a basis for selection and configuration.

**Demonstrator**
: A concretely runnable, exemplary implementation of a solution that makes its way of
  working tangible – not as a product, but as illustration and a basis for transfer.

**Data protection impact assessment (DPIA)**
: Procedure under the GDPR for systematically assessing and mitigating the risks of
  processing operations for data subjects.

**Works council**
: The elected representation of the employees of an establishment; when AI systems are
  introduced, co-determination rights exist under the Works Constitution Act.

**SMEs**
: Small and medium-sized enterprises – a core target group for DAISY because they usually
  have no IT security or data protection department of their own.

**Transdisciplinarity**
: A way of working that not only crosses disciplinary boundaries but involves practice
  partners in the research from the start. In the DAISY consortium: computer science,
  psychology and business practice.
