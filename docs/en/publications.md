# Publications & Results

This page brings together scientific publications and other results from the DAISY project, such as
talks, workshops, and proofs of concept and code, as they become available. Entries are listed
chronologically and in their original language.

The label above each title shows the **type of result**. The categories below the title indicate the
focus: **Computer Security**, **Psychology**, and **Human-Computer Interaction**. An entry may have
more than one category.
