# Project team

DAISY connects computer science, psychology and practice. The consortium comprises three
scientific partners and one practice partner; four associated partners contribute as
multipliers and dialogue partners without funding of their own.

Consortium lead and focus on the technical subsystem: security and data protection

## Associated partners

Associated partners involve the target groups and ensure the cross-sector transferability of
the results.

<div class="grid cards" markdown="1">

- __IG Metall__

    ---

    Multiplier and organized employee representation. Opens access to decision-makers in
    organizations – among other things via the AI conference for works councils as a
    platform for surveys and workshops. Communicates results to employees and works council
    members and turns competence-building measures into its own training offerings.

- __HUMAINE Network e.V.__

    ---

    Non-profit association and company network. Enables knowledge transfer on AIaaS
    strategies into industry, in particular into technology development and AI
    implementation in the Ruhr metropolitan region, and brings the findings into the
    nationwide dialogue on human-centered AIaaS solutions.

- __Edgeless Systems GmbH__

    ---

    Technology partner on the developer side. Examines the implementability of the
    protective measures developed in the project in its own products and thereby reflects
    the technical compatibility of the results with the market.

- __SBG Serious Business Gaming GmbH__

    ---

    Practice partner specializing in communicating data protection and cybersecurity
    through serious games. Supports the project's game-based awareness and science
    communication formats.
