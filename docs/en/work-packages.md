# Work Packages

The overall project is divided into five work packages (WPs). They comprise 43 sub-work
packages and contribute to 26 cross-project milestones. The project runs from
1 September 2026 to 31 August 2029. Here you find the overview level – the detailed tables
of the proposal have deliberately not been carried over.

| WP | Title | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| WP 1 | Framework conditions for AIaaS introductions | RUB |
| WP 2 | Exploring the mental models of decision-makers and employees | UDE |
| WP 3 | Comparing mental models with system characteristics | UW/H |
| WP 4 | Participatory derivation and implementation of solutions | RUB |
| WP 5 | Science communication and transfer | RUB |

## WP 1 – Framework conditions for AIaaS introductions

*Responsibility: Ruhr University Bochum (consortium coordination)*

The first work package creates the conceptual, scientific and legal foundations of the
project.

???+ info "Content"
    The three scientific partners review, in parallel and coordinated reviews, the technical
    (RUB), organizational-psychological (UW/H) and individual-psychological (UDE)
    literature and merge them into a joint review suitable for submission. In parallel,
    BoWE develops its internal AI strategy, informed by the needs of regional actors, and
    records the legal and practical problem areas in AIaaS introductions in companies.

    In addition, a legal opinion on the legal framework for AIaaS use is commissioned
    externally as a service contract – awarded via RUB and accompanied by it on a
    professional level.

## WP 2 – Exploring mental models

*Responsibility: University of Duisburg-Essen*

In the second work package, the mental models of employees as well as the decision-making
and selection processes of decision-makers are empirically collected and analyzed.

???+ info "Content"
    UDE uses qualitative interviews to investigate employees' mental models, UW/H the
    views and processes of decision-makers. In parallel, RUB prepares the technical
    characterization of practically relevant AIaaS systems and assesses them from a data
    protection perspective. BoWE arranges interview partners and models the internal AI
    decision-making processes as the current state.

## WP 3 – Comparison with actual system characteristics

*Responsibility: Witten/Herdecke University*

The third work package interlinks the technical and psychological findings.

???+ info "Content"
    RUB converts the recorded system characteristics into risk profiles and, together with
    the partners, derives technical requirements for data-protection-compliant
    configurations. UDE and UW/H work out the levers for calibrating mental models and the
    implications for the design of introduction processes, respectively. BoWE feeds the
    results back into the company's strategic considerations.

    With the process model for calibrated AIaaS implementation, UW/H is responsible for the
    integrating result of this work package – thereby fulfilling its role as a bridge between
    the social and the technical subsystem.

## WP 4 – Deriving, implementing and evaluating solutions

*Responsibility: Ruhr University Bochum*

In the fourth work package, the previously derived requirements are translated into concrete
solutions, implemented and evaluated.

???+ info "Content"
    RUB develops and evaluates the demonstrator, data-protection-compliant AIaaS
    configurations; UDE develops and implements measures to promote competence. UW/H analyzes
    organizational and fairness culture as a lever and compiles best-practice processes.
    BoWE implements the technical and organizational measures operationally – including
    change management – and evaluates them in practice.

    The exemplary implementation of data-protection-compliant AIaaS solutions by RUB forms
    the technical core of this work package.

## WP 5 – Science communication and transfer

*Responsibility: Ruhr University Bochum*

The fifth work package bundles the transfer and communication activities spread across the
entire project duration.

???+ info "Content"
    RUB creates this project website, turns the legal opinion into an application-oriented
    version, designs and coordinates the podcast series and prepares the developed solutions
    as interactive demonstrators. The partners contribute target-group-specific materials,
    podcast episodes, the online decision tree and visualized future scenarios; BoWE
    activates and supports regional actors. The conclusion is the joint compilation of the
    results into the final report.
